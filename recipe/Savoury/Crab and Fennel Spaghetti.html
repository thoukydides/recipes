Crab and Fennel Spaghetti

.cook
👥 2 servings 📊 Easy 🍽️ Main 🌍 Italian

🥘 Ingredients

  • black pepper (freshly ground)
  • cherry tomatoes (mixed colours)
    160 g
  • crabmeat (mixed brown and white)
    160 g
  • dried spaghetti
    150 g
  • fennel
    1 bulb
  • olive oil
    1 tbsp
  • parmesan (grated)
  • red chillies
    2
  • sea salt

🍳 Cookware

  • large non-stick frying pan
  1. 1
    Put a large non-stick frying pan on a medium-low heat. Trim the fennel , pick and reserve any leafy tops, then halve the bulb and finely slice it. Place in the pan with olive oil and cook with the lid on for ⏱️ 5 minutes . Meanwhile cook the dried spaghetti in a pan of boiling salted water, then drain.
    fennel: 1 bulb, olive oil: 1 tbsp, dried spaghetti: 150 g
  2. 2
    Deseed and finely slice the red chillies , stir into the fennel pan and cook uncovered until soft and sticky, stirring occasionally. Halve the cherry tomatoes and toss into the pan for ⏱️ 2 minutes , followed by the crabmeat for ⏱️ 1 minute , and finally the drained pasta.
    red chillies: 2, cherry tomatoes: 160 g (mixed colours), crabmeat: 160 g (mixed brown and white)
  3. 3
    Drizzle with olive oil , season with sea salt and black pepper , top with any reserved fennel tops, and dust with parmesan .
    olive oil, sea salt, black pepper (freshly ground), parmesan (grated)
