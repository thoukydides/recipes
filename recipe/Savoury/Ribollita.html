Ribollita

.cook
#vegetarian #savoury #main-course #italian

A delectable mishmash of vegetables, beans, cheese and stale bread, making it perfect for using up all sorts of odds and ends

👥 4 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 20m 🔥 Cook Time: 1h 🌍 Italian 🥗 Vegetarian 👤 Felicity Cloake 📖 www.theguardian.com

🥘 Ingredients

  • cannellini beans
    1 tin × 400 g
  • carrots (finely chopped)
    2
  • celery (finely chopped)
    2 sticks
  • chilli flakes
    1 tsp
  • chopped tomatoes
    1 tin × 400 g
  • fennel seeds
    1 tsp
  • garlic (peeled, squashed, and finely chopped)
    2 cloves
  • grated cheese
  • leek (thickly sliced)
    1
  • olive oil (optional)
  • pepper
  • red onions
    2
  • salt
    pinch
  • savoy cabbage outer leaves (or other greens) (shredded)
    250 g
  • spring onions (optional)
    2
  • squash (peeled and cut into rough 1 cm chunks)
    200 g
  • stale bread
    200 g
  • sunflower oil
    2 tbsp
  • wine, stock, or water
    250 ml

🍳 Cookware

  • large frying pan
  1. 📝

    This recipe is intended to use up leftovers. Ingredients can be substituted as required.

  2. 1
    Purée half of the cannellini beans with all of the liquid from the can.
    cannellini beans: 1 tin × 400 g
  3. 2
    Heat the sunflower oil in a large frying pan on a medium heat, then saute the red onions until they soften. Add the carrots , celery , and salt , and cook until soft.
    sunflower oil: 2 tbsp, red onions: 2, carrots: 2 (finely chopped), celery: 2 sticks (finely chopped), salt: pinch
  4. 3
    Add the leek , squash , savoy cabbage outer leaves (or other greens) , garlic , fennel seeds , and chilli flakes . Cover and cook until limp, stirring occasionally.
    leek: 1 (thickly sliced), squash: 200 g (peeled and cut into rough 1 cm chunks), savoy cabbage outer leaves (or other greens): 250 g (shredded), garlic: 2 cloves (peeled, squashed, and finely chopped), fennel seeds: 1 tsp, chilli flakes: 1 tsp
  5. 4
    Add the chopped tomatoes , puréed beans, and the wine, stock, or water . Cover again and leave to bubble for about ⏱️ 40 minutes , or until the vegetables are cooked to taste.
    chopped tomatoes: 1 tin × 400 g, wine, stock, or water: 250 ml
  6. 5
    Stir in the remaining beans and the stale bread . Cook until the latter is soft, then season to taste with salt and pepper .
    stale bread: 200 g, salt, pepper
  7. 6
    Optionally drizzle with olive oil and pop under a hot grill until crisp on top.
    olive oil
  8. 7
    Top with grated cheese and spring onions .
    grated cheese, spring onions: 2
