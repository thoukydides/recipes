Singaporean Curry Sauce

.cook
👥 2 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main 🌍 Singaporean

🥘 Ingredients

  • chicken stock (cut into 3-4 cm lengths)
    300 ml
  • cinnamon
    1 stick
  • coconut milk
    150 ml
  • curry powder
    1 1/2 tbsp
  • garlic
    2 cloves
  • ginger (peeled and finely chopped)
    0. thumb
  • green chillies (pierced with knife)
    3
  • light soy sauce
    1 tbsp
  • okra
    200 g
  • onion (finely diced)
    1
  • potato (cut into 5 cm chunks)
    1
  • salt
    1/2 tsp
  • sambal or chilli bean sauce
    1 tbsp
  • star anise
    2
  • vegetable oil
    2 tbsp
  1. 1
    Mix the coconut milk , sambal or chilli bean sauce , light soy sauce , and salt ingredients in a jug to make the sauce.
    coconut milk: 150 ml, sambal or chilli bean sauce: 1 tbsp, light soy sauce: 1 tbsp, salt: 1/2 tsp
  2. 2
    Heat vegetable oil in a saucepan to a medium heat. Fry the onion for 2 to ⏱️ 3 minutes . Add the ginger and garlic and fry for ⏱️ 1 minute or so.
    vegetable oil: 2 tbsp, onion: 1 (finely diced), ginger: 0. thumb (peeled and finely chopped), garlic: 2 cloves
  3. 3
    Then add all the spices together ( star anise , cinnamon , and curry powder ), the green chillies , and potato in turn, frying for about ⏱️ 1 minute after each addition.
    star anise: 2, cinnamon: 1 stick, curry powder: 1 1/2 tbsp, green chillies: 3 (pierced with knife), potato: 1 (cut into 5 cm chunks)
  4. 4
    Pour in a quarter of the sauce and bring to a vigorous boil. Add another quarter of the sauce and again bring to a boil, then repeat this process until all the sauce has been added. Bring to a boil once again.
  5. 5
    Pour in the chicken stock . Cook on a medium heat for ⏱️ 20 minutes .
    chicken stock: 300 ml (cut into 3-4 cm lengths)
  6. 6
    Add the okra . Cook for 5 to ⏱️ 10 minutes .
    okra: 200 g
  7. 📝

    Serve with rice.
