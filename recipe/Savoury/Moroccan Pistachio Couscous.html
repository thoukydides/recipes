Moroccan Pistachio Couscous

.cook
👥 4 servings 📊 Easy 🍽️ Side 🌍 Moroccan

🥘 Ingredients

  • couscous
    250 g
  • currants
    75 g
  • fresh mint (chopped)
    3 tbsp
  • garlic (crushed)
    2 cloves
  • hot chicken stock
    375 ml
  • lemon rind (grated)
    1
  • olive oil
    1 tbsp
  • orange rind (grated)
    1
  • pistachio nuts (shelled)
    100 g
  • red chilli (seeded and chopped)
    1
  • red onion (chopped)
    1
  • saffron
    pinch
  1. 1
    Add the saffron to the hot chicken stock and pour over the couscous . Set aside for ⏱️ 10 minutes , then stir to remove lumps.
    saffron: pinch, hot chicken stock: 375 ml, couscous: 250 g
  2. 2
    Heat the olive oil in a frying pan, add the red onion , red chilli and garlic and cook for ⏱️ 3 minutes . Add the currants and pistachio nuts and cook for ⏱️ 5 minutes . Stir in the lemon rind and orange rind and the fresh mint . Stir through the couscous.
    olive oil: 1 tbsp, red onion: 1 (chopped), red chilli: 1 (seeded and chopped), garlic: 2 cloves (crushed), currants: 75 g, pistachio nuts: 100 g (shelled), lemon rind: 1 (grated), orange rind: 1 (grated), fresh mint: 3 tbsp (chopped)
