Youvetsi (Greek Lamb Stew with Orzo)

.cook
📊 Medium 🍽️ Main 🌍 Greek

🥘 Ingredients

  • bay leaf
    1
  • black pepper
  • chopped tomatoes
    2 tins
  • cinnamon
    1/2 tsp
  • feta cheese
  • garlic (minced)
    4 cloves
  • lamb neck fillet, shoulder, or leg (cut into 1” chunks)
    0.5 kg
  • nutmeg
    1/2 tsp
  • olive oil
    3 tbsp
  • onions (finely chopped)
    2 large
  • oregano
    1 tsp
  • orzo pasta (or other small shapes)
    250 ml
  • paprika
    1 tsp
  • parsley (chopped)
    1/2 cup
  • red wine
    250 ml
  • salt
  • water
    250 ml

🍳 Cookware

  • large heavy pan with a lid
  1. 1
    Season the lamb neck fillet, shoulder, or leg with salt and black pepper .
    lamb neck fillet, shoulder, or leg: 0.5 kg (cut into 1” chunks), salt, black pepper
  2. 2
    In a large heavy pan with a lid , heat olive oil over medium-high heat until shimmering but not smoking. Add the lamb and cook for 7 to ⏱️ 10 minutes , tossing regularly, until browned. Transfer the lamb to a large plate.
    olive oil: 3 tbsp
  3. 3
    In the same pan, add the onions and garlic . Season with salt . Cook for 3 to ⏱️ 5 minutes , stirring regularly, until softened.
    onions: 2 large (finely chopped), garlic: 4 cloves (minced), salt
  4. 4
    Return the lamb to the pan. Add the red wine , oregano , paprika , cinnamon , nutmeg , and bay leaf . Cook until the wine has reduced by at least half, then add water and the chopped tomatoes . Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and let simmer for 45 minutes to ⏱️ 1 hour , or until the lamb is cooked through.
    red wine: 250 ml, oregano: 1 tsp, paprika: 1 tsp, cinnamon: 1/2 tsp, nutmeg: 1/2 tsp, bay leaf: 1, water: 250 ml, chopped tomatoes: 2 tins
  5. 📝

    Stop at this point if preparing the day before.

  6. 5
    Stir in the orzo pasta (or other small shapes) and cover the pan. Let it cook for another ⏱️ 20 minutes or until the orzo has cooked through and most of the moisture has been absorbed.
    orzo pasta (or other small shapes): 250 ml
  7. 6
    Garnish with parsley and crumbled feta cheese .
    parsley: 1/2 cup (chopped), feta cheese
