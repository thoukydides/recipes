Vindaloo

.cook
#savoury #curry #meat #indian #goan

A tangy Goan dish with sweet spices

👥 6 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 20m 🔥 Cook Time: 2h 🌍 Indian 👤 Felicity Cloake 📖 www.theguardian.com

🥘 Ingredients

Masala Marinade

  • Kashmiri chilli or paprika
    2 tbsp
  • beef shin, pork shoulder, or duck breast (cut into 3 cm chunks)
    700 g
  • black peppercorns
    1 tsp
  • cardamom pods (seeds only)
    8
  • cider vinegar
    75 ml
  • cinnamon
    1 tbsp
  • cloves
    8
  • coriander seeds
    1/2 tsp
  • cumin seeds
    1 tsp
  • turmeric
    1/2 tsp

Vindaloo

  • bird eye green chillies
    4
  • curry leaves
    10
  • garlic (finely sliced)
    10 cloves
  • ghee or sunflower oil
    4 tbsp
  • ginger (cut into slim matchsticks)
    5 cm
  • mustard seeds
    1 tsp
  • onions (finely sliced)
    4 medium
  • ripe tomatoes (diced)
    4
  • salt
    1 tsp
  • soft brown sugar
    1 tbsp
  • tamarind sauce
    60 g

Masala Marinade

  1. 1
    Grind together all the ingredients for the masala ( Kashmiri chilli or paprika , cardamom pods , black peppercorns , cloves , cumin seeds , coriander seeds , turmeric , and cinnamon ), then stir in the cider vinegar .
    Kashmiri chilli or paprika: 2 tbsp, cardamom pods: 8 (seeds only), black peppercorns: 1 tsp, cloves: 8, cumin seeds: 1 tsp, coriander seeds: 1/2 tsp, turmeric: 1/2 tsp, cinnamon: 1 tbsp, cider vinegar: 75 ml
  2. 2
    Rub into the beef shin, pork shoulder, or duck breast and leave to marinade for 3 to ⏱️ 4 hours .
    beef shin, pork shoulder, or duck breast: 700 g (cut into 3 cm chunks)

Vindaloo

  1. 1
    Heat the ghee or sunflower oil in a wide, lidded pan over a medium-low heat, and fry the onions until soft and golden.
    ghee or sunflower oil: 4 tbsp, onions: 4 medium (finely sliced)
  2. 2
    Stir the garlic and ginger into the onions and cook, stirring, for another ⏱️ 5 minutes . Add the ripe tomatoes , bird eye green chillies , and curry leaves . Cook until the tomatoes start to break down.
    garlic: 10 cloves (finely sliced), ginger: 5 cm (cut into slim matchsticks), ripe tomatoes: 4 (diced), bird eye green chillies: 4, curry leaves: 10
  3. 3
    Add the meat and marinade to the pan. Increase the heat to medium high. Stir well, add the soft brown sugar , salt , mustard seeds , and tamarind sauce . Bring to a simmer, then reduce to a low heat. Cover and cook gently for ⏱️ 1 hour .
    soft brown sugar: 1 tbsp, salt: 1 tsp, mustard seeds: 1 tsp, tamarind sauce: 60 g
  4. 4
    Remove the lid and cook for another ⏱️ 30 minutes , until the meat is very tender and the sauce has thickened.
