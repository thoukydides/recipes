Osaka-style Okonomiyaki - Cabbage “Noodle” Pancake

.cook
👥 8 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main 🌍 Japanese 📖 www.theguardian.com

🥘 Ingredients

Batter

  • eggs (beaten)
    2
  • plain flour
    400 g
  • salt
    pinch
  • spring onions (roughly chopped)
    4
  • water
    200 ml
  • white cabbage (very finely shredded, excluding core)
    400 g

Okonomiyaki

  • English mustard
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • ao nori (dried seaweed flakes)
  • beni-shoga (pickled red ginger)
  • kezuri-bushi / katsuobushi (dried shaved tuna flakes)
  • mayonnaise
  • raw prawns (heads and shells removed)
    225 g
  • scallops
    115 g
  • sunflower oil

Batter

  1. 1
    Make the batter by mixing the plain flour and water . Add extra water as necessary to make a smooth batter without lumps. Add the eggs and salt , and blend together. Add the spring onions and one third of the white cabbage , then mix well. Repeat until all of the cabbage is mixed in and evenly coated with the batter.
    plain flour: 400 g, water: 200 ml, eggs: 2 (beaten), salt: pinch, spring onions: 4 (roughly chopped), white cabbage: 400 g (very finely shredded, excluding core)

Okonomiyaki

  1. 1
    Heat the frying pan with a thin coating of sunflower oil until smoking, and reduce to a medium heat.
    sunflower oil
  2. 2
    Ladle some of the batter into the centre of the pan to form a circle about 1” thick and 4” diameter.
  3. 3
    Sprinkle the surface of the pancake with one-eighth of the raw prawns and scallops . Gently press down using a tablespoon.
    raw prawns: 225 g (heads and shells removed), scallops: 115 g
  4. 4
    When the edge of the pancake is cooked and the surface has started to try, turn it over using two spatulas. Gently press the pancake to stretch it to 6” diameter and half the thickness.
  5. 5
    After 2 to ⏱️ 3 minutes turn over again. If the surface is still wet then turn over and cook for another ⏱️ 2 minutes . While the pancake is still in the pan, topping side up, spread on some Worcestershire sauce , English mustard , and mayonnaise . Sprinkle with kezuri-bushi / katsuobushi and ao nori .
    Worcestershire sauce, English mustard, mayonnaise, kezuri-bushi / katsuobushi (dried shaved tuna flakes), ao nori (dried seaweed flakes)
  6. 6
    Keep the pancake warm while making seven more the same way.
  7. 7
    Serve hot with a sprinkling of beni-shoga on top.
    beni-shoga (pickled red ginger)
