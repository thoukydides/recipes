Sticky Prawns with Veg-Packed Egg Fried Rice

.cook
👥 4 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main 🌍 Chinese

🥘 Ingredients

  • eggs (beaten)
    4
  • garlic (finely grated)
    3 cloves
  • ginger (peeled and finely chopped)
    25 g
  • honey
    1 tsp
  • mixed stir fry vegetables
    330 g
  • raw king prawns
    330 g
  • rice (jasmine or long grain)
    250 g
  • salt
    pinch
  • sesame oil
    5 tbsp
  • sesame seeds
  • soy sauce
    3 tbsp
  • spring onions
    6
  • sriracha (or other hot sauce)
    3 tbsp

🍳 Cookware

  • large wok
  • frying pan
  1. 1
    Rinse and cook the rice . When cooked rinse thoroughly under cold water to remove any starch. Set aside to cool.
    rice: 250 g (jasmine or long grain)
  2. 2
    Heat 1 tablespoon of sesame oil in a large wok . Fry the raw king prawns on a medium-high heat for 1 or ⏱️ 2 minutes on each side, until just turned pink. Transfer to a plate or bowl and set aside for later.
    sesame oil: 5 tbsp, raw king prawns: 330 g
  3. 3
    Return the wok to a high heat and add 2 tablespoons of sesame oil. Fry the white parts of the spring onions and the mixed stir fry vegetables for ⏱️ 2 minutes to soften.
    spring onions: 6, mixed stir fry vegetables: 330 g
  4. 4
    Add the rice and 2 tablespoons of soy sauce . Cook this mixture for ⏱️ 2 minutes , stirring often to coat the rice with the soy sauce.
    soy sauce: 3 tbsp
  5. 5
    Move the rice and vegetables to the sides of the wok. Add a drizzle of oil and pour in the eggs . Leave for a ⏱️ 1 minute before beating again, then stir vigorously through the rice so that the egg breaks up among the grains.
    eggs: 4 (beaten)
  6. 6
    Add salt and most of the green sliced spring onions (reserving a handful for garnish) to the pan, then turn the heat down very low.
    salt: pinch
  7. 7
    In a clean frying pan , heat another tablespoon of oil over a medium-high heat. Add the garlic and ginger . Cook for ⏱️ 2 minutes , stirring so it does not burn, until just beginning to colour.
    garlic: 3 cloves (finely grated), ginger: 25 g (peeled and finely chopped)
  8. 8
    Add the sriracha , honey , and remaining soy sauce. Let it all sizzle for a few minutes, stirring occasionally, until looking sticky.
    sriracha: 3 tbsp (or other hot sauce), honey: 1 tsp
  9. 9
    Add the prawns to the sauce and gently shake the pan to coat. Once coated, turn down the heat.
  10. 10
    Divide the egg fried rice between 4 bowls and top each one with the sticky glazed prawns, a sprinkling of sesame seeds , a splash of soy sauce and sriracha, then the remaining green spring onion slices.
    sesame seeds
