Singapore Noodles v1

.cook
#savoury #main-course #seafood #cantonese #chinese

Simple Singapore noodles, good as an accompaniment for seared salmon

👥 4 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 10m 🔥 Cook Time: 10m 🌍 Cantonese 👤 Nigella Lawson 📖 Nigella Express <urn:isbn:9780701181840>

🥘 Ingredients

  • Chinese cooking wine (or sherry)
    125 ml
  • Chinese leaf (finely sliced)
    100 g
  • Madras curry powder, medium
    2 tsp
  • Seared Salmon
  • baby corn (sliced into thin rounds)
    125 g
  • bean sprouts
    150 g
  • chicken stock
    250 ml
  • dried shrimp (or 100 g small frozen prawns)
    50 g
  • fresh coriander (chopped)
    4 tbsp
  • fresh ginger (finely chopped)
    1 tsp
  • garlic oil
    1 tbsp
  • soy sauce
    3 tbsp
  • spring onions (finely sliced)
    2
  • vermicelli rice noodles
    250 g

🍳 Cookware

  • wok
  1. 1
    Put the vermicelli rice noodles into a bowl and cover with boiling water. Leave them to soak for ⏱️ 4 minutes and then drain them.
    vermicelli rice noodles: 250 g
  2. 2
    Soak the dried shrimp in the Chinese cooking wine (or sherry) , then heat the garlic oil in a wok and fry the Chinese leaf , baby corn , and spring onions for a ⏱️ 2 minutes .
    dried shrimp: 50 g (or 100 g small frozen prawns), Chinese cooking wine (or sherry): 125 ml, garlic oil: 1 tbsp, Chinese leaf: 100 g (finely sliced), baby corn: 125 g (sliced into thin rounds), spring onions: 2 (finely sliced)
  3. 3
    Add the Madras curry powder, medium and fresh ginger to the wok, and then the chicken stock and soy sauce . Pour in the shrimps, with their wine, and the drained, soaked noodles, tossing and shaking everything all together in the wok.
    Madras curry powder, medium: 2 tsp, fresh ginger: 1 tsp (finely chopped), chicken stock: 250 ml, soy sauce: 3 tbsp
  4. 4
    Finally, stir in the bean sprouts and give a final toss, before turning out into a bowl and sprinkling with the fresh coriander . Serve with Seared Salmon .
    bean sprouts: 150 g, fresh coriander: 4 tbsp (chopped), Seared Salmon
