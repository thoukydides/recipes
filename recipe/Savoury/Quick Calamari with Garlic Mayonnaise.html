Quick Calamari with Garlic Mayonnaise

.cook
#seafood #savoury #starter

Simple but moorish shallow-fried squid rings

👥 4 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Starter ⏱️ Prep Time: 15m 🔥 Cook Time: 15m 👤 Nigella Lawson 📖 Nigella Express <urn:isbn:9780701181840>

🥘 Ingredients

Garlic Mayonnaise

  • garlic
    1/2 clove
  • mayonnaise
    100 g

Calamari

  • cornflour
    2 tbsp
  • frozen squid, tubes and tentacles
    500 g
  • groundnut oil (or as needed depending on size of pan)
    250 ml
  • paprika
    1 tsp
  • salt
    1 tsp
  • semolina
    4 tbsp
  1. 📝

    Double quantities to serve as a main course.

Garlic Mayonnaise

  1. 1
    Grate or crush the garlic into the mayonnaise and stir to mix.
    garlic: 1/2 clove, mayonnaise: 100 g

Calamari

  1. 1
    Heat the groundnut oil in a smallish saucepan and, while it is left to warm up, cut the thawed frozen squid, tubes and tentacles into 1 cm rings.
    groundnut oil: 250 ml (or as needed depending on size of pan), frozen squid, tubes and tentacles: 500 g
  2. 2
    Put the cornflour , semolina , salt , and paprika into a plastic freezer bag.
    cornflour: 2 tbsp, semolina: 4 tbsp, salt: 1 tsp, paprika: 1 tsp
  3. 3
    Add the squid rings and tentacles and then toss to coat.
  4. 4
    When the oil is hot enough, which is when it sizzles up fiercely when you drop in a small cube of bread, fry the squid in batches to get the most golden crunchiness. ⏱️ 2 minutes per batch is all you should need.
