Pizza Dough

.cook
#italian #ingredient #main-course #vegetarian #savoury

A simple dough for crispy pizzas

👥 6 servings 📊 Easy 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 30m 🔥 Cook Time: 5m 🌍 Italian 🥗 Vegetarian 👤 Jamie Oliver 📖 www.jamieoliver.com

🥘 Ingredients

  • Tipo '00' or strong white flour
    1 kg
  • caster sugar
    1 tbsp
  • coarse semolina
  • fresh yeast (or 14 g dried yeast)
    30 g
  • lukewarm water
    650 ml
  • olive oil
    4 tbsp
  • salt
    1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • pizza stone
  1. 📝

    Tesco sell fresh yeast in multiples of 100 g.

  2. 1
    Sieve the Tipo '00' or strong white flour and salt on to a clean work surface and make a well in the middle. In a jug mix the fresh yeast , caster sugar and olive oil into the lukewarm water and leave for a few minutes, then pour into the well. Using a fork bring the flour in gradually from the sides and swirl it into the liquid. Keep mixing, drawing larger amounts of flour in. When it all starts to come together work the rest of the flour in with flour-dusted hands. Knead until dough is smooth and springy.
    Tipo '00' or strong white flour: 1 kg, salt: 1 tsp, fresh yeast: 30 g (or 14 g dried yeast), caster sugar: 1 tbsp, olive oil: 4 tbsp, lukewarm water: 650 ml
  3. 2
    Place the ball of dough in a very large flour-dusted bowl (with lots of room for expansion) and flour the top of it. Cover the bowl with a damp cloth and place in a warm room for about an hour until the dough has doubled in size.
  4. 3
    Remove the dough to a flour-dusted surface and knead it a bit to push the air out.
  5. 4
    Either use immediately or keep in fridge/freezer wrapped in clingfilm until needed.
  6. 5
    Pre-heat the pizza stone for ⏱️ 30 minutes on level 2 at the oven's highest temperature.
  7. 6
    Liberally dust the worktop with coarse semolina and roll-out the pizzas about 15 to ⏱️ 20 minutes before cooking them. Do not put too much topping (especially tomato purée and cheese) in order to prevent the middle staying soggy. Cook at the top of the hottest oven for ⏱️ 5 minutes until the base turns golden brown.
