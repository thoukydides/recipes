French Onion Soup

.cook
👥 4 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Starter 🌍 French

🥘 Ingredients

  • French bread (made into croutons)
  • Gruyère cheese (grated)
    100 g
  • beef stock
    855 ml
  • butter
    30 g
  • fresh parsley (chopped)
  • garlic
    3 cloves
  • onions
    6 medium
  • pepper
  • red wine
    1 glass
  • salt
  • sugar
    1 tsp
  1. 1
    Peel and slice the onions and garlic . Melt the butter and add the onions, garlic and sugar , stirring constantly over a moderate heat until the onions brown and soften. If the onions start to stick, reduce the heat.
    onions: 6 medium, garlic: 3 cloves, butter: 30 g, sugar: 1 tsp
  2. 2
    When the onions are uniformly caramelized (after 10 to ⏱️ 15 minutes ), add the red wine and boil for 2 to ⏱️ 3 minutes to reduce it.
    red wine: 1 glass
  3. 3
    Add the beef stock and simmer for a further ⏱️ 10 minutes . Add the salt and pepper to taste.
    beef stock: 855 ml, salt, pepper
  4. 4
    Serve topped with toasted French bread , topped with Gruyère cheese and a sprinkling of fresh parsley . To melt the cheese place soup in heatproof bowls under the grill for a few minutes before serving.
    French bread (made into croutons), Gruyère cheese: 100 g (grated), fresh parsley (chopped)
