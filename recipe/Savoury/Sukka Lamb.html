Sukka Lamb

.cook
#indian #main-course #savoury #meat

A very tasty dry curry, inspired by Cottenham Curry Palace

👥 6 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 30m 🔥 Cook Time: 3h45m 🌍 Indian 👤 Alexander Thoukydides

🥘 Ingredients

Braise

  • black pepper (freshly ground)
    1 tsp
  • garam masala
    1 tsp
  • garlic
    10 cloves
  • ginger (finely chopped)
    2 inch
  • ground coriander
    4 tsp
  • ground cumin
    2 tsp
  • lamb leg (cut into large chunks)
    1 kg
  • onion (finely chopped)
    1 medium
  • salt
    pinch
  • turmeric
    2 tsp
  • water
    100 ml
  • yoghurt
    3 tbsp

Stir Fry

  • bell peppers (cut into thick strips)
    3
  • finger chillies (cut diagonally, seeds left in)
    20
  • garam masala
    1/2 tsp
  • ground coriander
    1 tsp
  • ground cumin
    1/2 tsp
  • mustard powder
    1 tsp
  • onions (cut into wedges)
    3 medium
  • vegetable oil
    3 tbsp

🍳 Cookware

  • casserole dish with lid

Braise

  1. 1
    Place the lamb leg , onion , ginger , and garlic in a casserole dish with lid .
    lamb leg: 1 kg (cut into large chunks), onion: 1 medium (finely chopped), ginger: 2 inch (finely chopped), garlic: 10 cloves
  2. 2
    Mix the spices ( turmeric , ground coriander , ground cumin , black pepper , garam masala , and salt ) into the water and yoghurt .
    turmeric: 2 tsp, ground coriander: 4 tsp, ground cumin: 2 tsp, black pepper: 1 tsp (freshly ground), garam masala: 1 tsp, salt: pinch, water: 100 ml, yoghurt: 3 tbsp
  3. 3
    Pour the sauce over the lamb, fit the lid, and simmer at 150°C for 2.5 to ⏱️ 3 hours until the lamb is incredibly tender.
  4. 4
    Remove the lid and let most of the remaining liquid bubble for ⏱️ 30 minutes .

Stir Fry

  1. 1
    Heat the vegetable oil in a medium-hot frying pan. Add the bell peppers and finger chillies , and stir-fry for ⏱️ 2 minutes .
    vegetable oil: 3 tbsp, bell peppers: 3 (cut into thick strips), finger chillies: 20 (cut diagonally, seeds left in)
  2. 2
    Add the onions and spices ( ground coriander , ground cumin , garam masala , and mustard powder ), and fry for ⏱️ 15 seconds .
    onions: 3 medium (cut into wedges), ground coriander: 1 tsp, ground cumin: 1/2 tsp, garam masala: 1/2 tsp, mustard powder: 1 tsp
  3. 3
    Add the lamb with all remaining liquid and increase the heat to high. Stir fry until dry and the meat is glazed and browned.
