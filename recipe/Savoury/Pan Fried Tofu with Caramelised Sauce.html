Pan Fried Tofu with Caramelised Sauce

.cook
👥 4 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Started

🥘 Ingredients

Drain Tofu

  • tofu (2 blocks)
    570 g

Prepare Marinade

  • mirin
    4 tbsp
  • sake
    4 tbsp
  • soy sauce
    4 tbsp
  • spring onions
    4

Fry Garlic Chips

  • garlic
    4 cloves
  • vegetable oil
    2 tsp

Fry and Serve Tofu

  • butter (cut into 5 equal pieces)
    50 g
  • watercress

Drain Tofu

  1. 1
    Drain the tofu and wrap in three layers of kitchen paper. Put a large plate or chopping board on top as a weight. Leave for ⏱️ 30 minutes to allow time for the excess liquid to be absorbed by the paper.
    tofu: 570 g (2 blocks)

Prepare Marinade

  1. 1
    Chop the spring onions finely. Mix with the sake , soy sauce , and mirin in a shallow bowl. Leave for 15 minutes
    spring onions: 4, sake: 4 tbsp, soy sauce: 4 tbsp, mirin: 4 tbsp

Fry Garlic Chips

  1. 1
    Slice the garlic very thinly to make garlic chips. Heat the vegetable oil in a frying pan and fry the garlic for a few moments until golden. Turn the chips frequently to prevent sticking and burning. Scoop them out onto kitchen paper. Reserve the oil in the pan.
    garlic: 4 cloves, vegetable oil: 2 tsp

Marinade Tofu

  1. 1
    Unwrap the tofu and cut into 2 cm cubes. Soak in the marinade for about ⏱️ 15 minutes .

Fry and Serve Tofu

  1. 1
    Take out the tofu and wipe off the excess marinade with kitchen paper. Reserve the marinade.
  2. 2
    Reheat the oil in the frying pan and add one piece of butter . When the oil starts sizzling, reduce the heat to medium and add the pieces of tofu one by one. Cook in one layer, if possible.
    butter: 50 g (cut into 5 equal pieces)
  3. 3
    Cover and cook until the edge of the tofu is browned and quite firm, approximately 5-8 minutes on each side. (If the edges burn but the centre is pale, reduce the heat.)
  4. 4
    Pour the marinade into the pan. Cook for 2 minutes, or until the spring onion is very soft. Remove the tofu and arrange on each serving plate. Pour the thickened marinade and spring onion mixture over the tofu and top with a piece of butter. Sprinkle with the garlic chips and garnish with watercress . Serve hot.
    watercress
