Kleftiko v2

.cook
👥 6 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 30m 🔥 Cook Time: 5h15m 🌍 Greek 📖 www.theguardian.com

🥘 Ingredients

  • bay leaf
    1
  • cherry tomatoes
    12
  • dried oregano
    1 tsp
  • garlic (cut in half laterally)
    1 1/2 head
  • ground cinnamon
    1 tsp
  • lamb shoulder
    2 kg
  • lemon
    1
  • lemon juice
    2 tbsp
  • olive oil
  • red onion (cut into wedges)
    1 large
  • red pepper (cut into chunky strips)
    1
  • salt
    1 tsp
  • water
    200 ml
  • waxy potatoes (cut into wedges)
    1 kg
  1. 1
    Roughly crush garlic and rub over the lamb shoulder . Pour the olive oil and lemon juice over the meat, and sprinkle with the ground cinnamon , dried oregano , and salt . Rub all of the ingredients into the meat and leave for ⏱️ 12 hours .
    garlic: 1/2 head, lamb shoulder: 2 kg, olive oil, lemon juice: 2 tbsp, ground cinnamon: 1 tsp, dried oregano: 1 tsp, salt: 1 tsp
  2. 2
    Heat oven to 160°C.
  3. 3
    Place two pieces of greaseproof paper at right-angles into the base of a roasting pan, large enough to fold over the top of the joint. Use a roasting pan that is shorter than the paper width so that the liquid will be contained when wrapped.
  4. 4
    Line the base of the roasting tin with the waxy potatoes , red onion , red pepper , and cherry tomatoes . Add the bay leaf , and the garlic . Squeeze the lemon over the vegetables and add its shells. Pour water into the dish.
    waxy potatoes: 1 kg (cut into wedges), red onion: 1 large (cut into wedges), red pepper: 1 (cut into chunky strips), cherry tomatoes: 12, bay leaf: 1, garlic: 1 head (cut in half laterally), lemon: 1, water: 200 ml
  5. 5
    Place the lamb shoulder on top of the vegetables. Sprinkle the overhanging greaseproof paper with water and fold over to form a sealed package.
  6. 6
    Bake for 4 to ⏱️ 5 hours until very tender.
  7. 7
    Turn the oven up to 200°C and roast uncovered for 10 to ⏱️ 15 minutes .
  8. 8
    Remove the lamb shoulder and set aside. Roast the vegetables for a further 15 minutes until starting to brown, then serve with the meat.
