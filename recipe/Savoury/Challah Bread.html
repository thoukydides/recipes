Challah Bread

.cook
#bread #jewish #savoury #vegetarian

Jewish plaited egg bread, typically eaten on ceremonial occasions such as Shabbat and major Jewish holidays

📊 Medium 🍽️ Bread ⏱️ Prep Time: 30m 🔥 Cook Time: 25m 🌍 Jewish 🥗 Vegetarian 👤 Lorna Walker & Joyce Hughes 📖 The Complete Bread Book <urn:isbn:0600314146>

🥘 Ingredients

  • caster sugar
    50 g
  • egg
    2
  • fresh yeast
    30 g
  • plain flour
    600 g
  • poppy seeds
  • salt
    1 tsp
  • vegetable oil
    60 ml
  • warm water (hand-hot)
    300 ml
  • water
    a little

🍳 Cookware

  • very large bowl
  • clean cloth
  1. 1
    In a large mixing bowl dissolve the fresh yeast in 120 ml of the warm water . Add the caster sugar , vegetable oil , salt , and egg , and mix together well. Then add the remaining water.
    fresh yeast: 30 g, warm water: 300 ml (hand-hot), caster sugar: 50 g, vegetable oil: 60 ml, salt: 1 tsp, egg: 1
  2. 2
    Slowly stir in the plain flour and mix into a soft dough. Turn out onto a floured board and knead well for 5 minutes. Shape into a very large bowl (with lots of room for expansion), cover with a clean cloth , and leave in a warm place for ⏱️ 2 hours .
    plain flour: 600 g
  3. 3
    Divide the dough into two balls. Put one aside and cover with a clean cloth until you are ready to work with it and divide the other into four smaller balls. Shape each ball into long strands. It is easiest to make the strands in stages - first taking each ball to 6 inch, then 9 inch, and then to the full 12 inch length.
  4. 4
    To plait the four strands, pinch them all together at one end, then spread them out to form a double V-shape. Now thread the strands right-over-left for the pair on the right, and right-over-left for the pair on the left. Then for the pair in the middle take the left strand over the right one. Repeat this sequence until the end of the strands, then pinch them all together and tuck the join neatly under the end of the loaf.
  5. 5
    Lightly beat the second egg with water and glaze both loaves. Sprinkle poppy seeds on top.
    egg: 1, water: a little, poppy seeds
  6. 6
    Place in a cold oven and then heat the oven to 160°C. Bake for ⏱️ 25 minutes , checking from time to time. The loaves are done when they are nicely browned on top and give off a hollow sound when tapped on the bottom. Leave to cool on a wire rack.
