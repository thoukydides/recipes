Vietnamese Style Sticky Shaking Beef

.cook
👥 2 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main 🌍 Vietnamese

🥘 Ingredients

Marinade Beef

  • black pepper (ground)
    1 tsp
  • dark soy sauce
    1 tbsp
  • fish sauce
    1 tbsp
  • garlic (finely chopped)
    2 cloves
  • rump steak (cut into bite-sized cubes)
    500 g
  • sugar
    2 tsp

Sauce

  • oyster sauce
    4 tbsp
  • sugar
    1 tbsp
  • water
    4 tbsp
  • white wine vinegar
    4 tbsp

Stir Fry Beef

  • cornflour (mixed to a paste with a little water)
    1 tbsp
  • red onions (finely sliced)
    2

Marinade Beef

  1. 1
    Put the rump steak into a bowl with the marinade ingredients ( garlic , dark soy sauce , fish sauce , sugar , and black pepper ). Stir well and then set aside for ⏱️ 20 minutes .
    rump steak: 500 g (cut into bite-sized cubes), garlic: 2 cloves (finely chopped), dark soy sauce: 1 tbsp, fish sauce: 1 tbsp, sugar: 2 tsp, black pepper: 1 tsp (ground)

Sauce

  1. 1
    Combine the sauce ingredients ( white wine vinegar , oyster sauce , water , sugar ) in a small bowl and set aside.
    white wine vinegar: 4 tbsp, oyster sauce: 4 tbsp, water: 4 tbsp, sugar: 1 tbsp

Stir Fry Beef

  1. 1
    Heat a splash of oil in a non-stick frying pan over a high heat. When hot, add the marinated beef. Stir fry for ⏱️ 2 minutes until it begins to caramelise.
  2. 2
    Add three quarters of the red onions to the pan and stir fry for ⏱️ 1 minute .
    red onions: 2 (finely sliced)
  3. 3
    Pour in the sauce and cook for a further ⏱️ 2 minutes .
  4. 4
    Turn down the heat and quickly stir in the cornflour to thicken the sauce, making sure there are no lumps.
    cornflour: 1 tbsp (mixed to a paste with a little water)
  5. 5
    Serve the sticky beef with rice and the remaining red onion scattered on top.
