Coffee, Cocoa and Raisin Loaf

.cook
📊 Hard 🍽️ Side ⏱️ Prep Time: 1h 🔥 Cook Time: 50m 📖 www.siemens-home.bsh-group.com

🥘 Ingredients

Poolish (yeast pre-ferment)

  • fast acting dried yeast
    3.5 g
  • malted (granary) flour
    100 g
  • strong white flour
    100 g
  • water
    250 ml

Espresso Raisins

  • espresso (2 × espresso doppio)
    180 ml
  • raisins
    200 g

Dough

  • cocoa powder (sieved)
    40 g
  • fast acting dried yeast
    3.5 g
  • malted (granary) flour
    180 g
  • rice flour
  • salt
    12 g
  • strong white flour
    450 g
  • water
    300 ml

🍳 Cookware

  • universal pan
  • pizza peel

Poolish (yeast pre-ferment)

  1. 1
    For the poolish, mix the strong white flour , malted (granary) flour , fast acting dried yeast , and water in a large bowl. Leave at room temperature for 12 to ⏱️ 24 hours .
    strong white flour: 100 g, malted (granary) flour: 100 g, fast acting dried yeast: 3.5 g, water: 250 ml

Espresso Raisins

  1. 1
    Let the espresso cool slightly, and then add sufficient raisins until just covered. Leave to soak for 12 to ⏱️ 24 hours .
    espresso: 180 ml (2 × espresso doppio), raisins: 200 g

Dough

  1. 1
    The next day, for the dough mix together the strong white flour , malted (granary) flour , cocoa powder , salt , and fast acting dried yeast , until combined. Add the poolish and 200 ml of the water (but not the espresso soaked raisins). Knead on a low speed for about ⏱️ 5 minutes until a smooth dough starts to form. Add the soaked raisins, and (if necessary) some of the remaining water. Knead for a further ⏱️ 3 minutes until smooth.
    strong white flour: 450 g, malted (granary) flour: 180 g, cocoa powder: 40 g (sieved), salt: 12 g, fast acting dried yeast: 3.5 g, water: 300 ml
  2. 2
    Transfer to a clean bowl and place in a warm place (e.g. oven set to 35°C) for ⏱️ 30 minutes or until doubled in size.
  3. 3
    Turn the dough out onto an un-floured surface and knock back slightly. Lightly dust the top with rice flour and turn over. Shape into a boule by folding each corner into the centre to create a seam. Then turn the dough over, using a bench scraper to help rotate the dough around to create surface tension.
    rice flour
  4. 4
    Line a clean bowl with a tea towel and generously dust with rice flour . Place the shaped loaf into this bowl and leave to rise for a further ⏱️ 25 minutes until doubled in size again.
    rice flour
  5. 5
    Preheat the oven to 220°C with a universal pan set at level 2, and a dish of water on the base of the oven. Once preheated, turn the risen loaf onto a piece of baking parchment over a pizza peel . Score the top of the loaf with a serrated knife, to create the “ear” of the loaf.
  6. 6
    Carefully slide the loaf onto the preheated tray and reduce the oven temperature to 180°C. Bake for 40 to ⏱️ 50 minutes until golden and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped on the base.
