Lamb Doner

.cook
👥 2 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 20m 🔥 Cook Time: 10m 🌍 Greek

🥘 Ingredients

Lamb Doner

  • bicarbonate of soda
    1/2 tsp
  • black pepper (freshly ground)
  • dried chilli flakes
    pinch
  • dried oregano
    1 tsp
  • garlic (finely grated)
    2 cloves
  • ground cumin
    2 tsp
  • ground paprika
    1 tsp
  • lamb leg meat (fat removed)
    175 g
  • lamb mince 20% fat
    175 g
  • onion powder
    1 tsp
  • sea salt
    1/2 tsp

Yoghurt Sauce

  • mint leaves (finely chopped)
    2 tbsp
  • plain yoghurt 0% fat
    100 ml
  • salt
    pinch
  • sugar
    pinch

Serve

  • cabbage (shredded)
    40 g
  • corn tortillas
    2
  • cucumber (thinly sliced)
    50 g
  • hot sauce
  • pickled chillies
    6
  • red onion (thinly sliced)
    1/2
  • tomatoes (thinly sliced)
    2

🍳 Cookware

  • food processor
  • blowtorch

Lamb Doner

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  2. 2
    Place all of the doner ingredients ( lamb mince 20% fat , lamb leg meat , garlic , sea salt , bicarbonate of soda , ground cumin , ground paprika , onion powder , dried oregano , dried chilli flakes , and black pepper ) into a food processor and pulse until smooth. Divide the mixture in half. Shape each portion into a ball and roll out between two large sheets of baking paper, until very thin. Remove the top sheet of baking paper from each piece of meat. Bake for 3 to ⏱️ 4 minutes until browned.
    lamb mince 20% fat: 175 g, lamb leg meat: 175 g (fat removed), garlic: 2 cloves (finely grated), sea salt: 1/2 tsp, bicarbonate of soda: 1/2 tsp, ground cumin: 2 tsp, ground paprika: 1 tsp, onion powder: 1 tsp, dried oregano: 1 tsp, dried chilli flakes: pinch, black pepper (freshly ground)
  3. 3
    Remove the trays from the oven and carefully use a blowtorch over the surface of the lamb until blackened in small areas. Leave to rest for a couple of minutes.

Yoghurt Sauce

  1. 1
    Mix together the plain yoghurt 0% fat , mint leaves , sugar , and salt , and set aside.
    plain yoghurt 0% fat: 100 ml, mint leaves: 2 tbsp (finely chopped), sugar: pinch, salt: pinch

Serve

  1. 1
    Warm the corn tortillas on the top shelf of the oven for ⏱️ 1 minute and place between two warmed plates.
    corn tortillas: 2
  2. 2
    Slice the lamb into strips, about 2.5cm wide. Serve the lamb on the open tortillas and top with the serving ingredients ( cabbage , cucumber , tomatoes , red onion , and pickled chillies ), yoghurt sauce and hot sauce , if using. Fill generously until unable to wrap.
    cabbage: 40 g (shredded), cucumber: 50 g (thinly sliced), tomatoes: 2 (thinly sliced), red onion: 1/2 (thinly sliced), pickled chillies: 6, hot sauce
