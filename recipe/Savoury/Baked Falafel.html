Baked Falafel

.cook
👥 3 servings 📊 Easy 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 30m 🔥 Cook Time: 1h 🌍 Egyptian 📖 www.bbc.co.uk

🥘 Ingredients

  • Tzatziki
  • black pepper (freshly ground)
  • chickpeas (rinsed and drained)
    2 tins × 400 g
  • coriander leaves (chopped)
    30 g
  • cumin (ground)
    3 tsp
  • flat leaf parsley leaves (chopped)
    30 g
  • garlic (grated)
    4 cloves
  • ground coriander
    2 tsp
  • olive oil
    1 tsp
  • onions
    2 small
  • plain flour
    40 g
  • sea salt flakes
    2 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • baking tray
  • small frying pan
  • food processor
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 210°C and line a baking tray with baking parchment.
  2. 2
    Heat the olive oil in a small frying pan over a medium heat. Add the onions and cook for about ⏱️ 10 minutes , or until softened and starting to brown (add a splash of water if they start to stick). Remove from the heat and leave to cool.
    olive oil: 1 tsp, onions: 2 small
  3. 3
    Put the onions, chickpeas , garlic , cumin , ground coriander , sea salt flakes , coriander leaves , flat leaf parsley leaves , plain flour , and some black pepper in a food processor . Pulse until the mixture is fairly smooth, stopping every so often to scrape down the sides with a spatula. Retain some texture but the paste should be able to hold together.
    chickpeas: 2 tins × 400 g (rinsed and drained), garlic: 4 cloves (grated), cumin: 3 tsp (ground), ground coriander: 2 tsp, sea salt flakes: 2 tsp, coriander leaves: 30 g (chopped), flat leaf parsley leaves: 30 g (chopped), plain flour: 40 g, black pepper (freshly ground)
  4. 4
    Divide the mixture into 18 equal pieces and shape into patties. Place on the prepared baking tray and spray or brush the patties with olive oil . Cook on the top shelf of the oven for 20 to ⏱️ 25 minutes , or until golden-brown.
    olive oil
  5. 5
    Reduce the oven temperature to 180°C and move the tray of falafel to the bottom shelf. Bake for a further 20 to ⏱️ 25 minutes , or until cooked through. Serve with Tzatziki .
    Tzatziki
