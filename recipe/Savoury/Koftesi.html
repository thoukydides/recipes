Koftesi (Grilled Chicken with Yoghurt, Hot Pepper, and Garlic)

.cook
📊 Medium 🍽️ Main 🌍 Turkish

🥘 Ingredients

  • Aleppo pepper
    2 tbsp
  • black pepper (freshly ground)
    1 tsp
  • chicken thighs or breast, skinless and boneless (cut into 1⨉1⨉½” pieces)
    700 g
  • coarse salt
    1 1/2 tsp
  • garlic (peeled and crushed)
    6 cloves
  • greek yoghurt
    235 ml
  • lemon (thinly sliced and seeds removed)
    1
  • olive oil
    3 tbsp
  • red wine vinegar
    2 tbsp
  • tomato purée
    2 tbsp
  • warm water
    1 tbsp
  1. 1
    Place Aleppo pepper in a small bowl and add warm water . Let stand for about ⏱️ 5 minutes until a thick paste forms.
    Aleppo pepper: 1 tbsp, warm water: 1 tbsp
  2. 2
    Place the pepper paste in a large nonreactive bowl. Add the greek yoghurt , olive oil , red wine vinegar , tomato purée , coarse salt and black pepper . Whisk to mix. Stir in the garlic and lemon .
    greek yoghurt: 235 ml, olive oil: 3 tbsp, red wine vinegar: 2 tbsp, tomato purée: 2 tbsp, coarse salt: 1 1/2 tsp, black pepper: 1 tsp (freshly ground), garlic: 6 cloves (peeled and crushed), lemon: 1 (thinly sliced and seeds removed)
  3. 3
    Add the chicken thighs or breast, skinless and boneless to the marinade and stir to coat evenly. Let the chicken marinate in the refrigerator, covered, for at least 1 hour or as long as 4. The longer the chicken marinates, the richer the flavour will be.
    chicken thighs or breast, skinless and boneless: 700 g (cut into 1⨉1⨉½” pieces)
  4. 4
    Thread the chicken through the thin side onto skewers so that the flat side of the chicken will be exposed to the fire.
  5. 5
    When ready to cook sprinkle each chicken kebab on both sides with coarse salt , black pepper , and the remaining Aleppo pepper . Grill until nicely browned and cooked through, 2 to ⏱️ 4 minutes per side.
    coarse salt, black pepper, Aleppo pepper: 1 tbsp
