Avgolemono Soup

.cook
👥 4 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 30m 🔥 Cook Time: 1h30m 🌍 Greek 📖 www.theguardian.com

🥘 Ingredients

Stock Base

  • black peppercorns
    1 tsp
  • carrot (roughly chopped)
    1
  • chicken (jointed, plus carcas)
    1 small
  • garlic (peeled)
    1 clove
  • olive oil
    2 tbsp
  • onion (roughly chopped)
    1
  • thyme (optional)
    1 sprig
  • tomato (whole) (optional)
    1 medium
  • water
    2.25 litres

Soup

  • carrot (diced)
    1
  • eggs
    2
  • leek (sliced)
    1 slender
  • lemon juice (optional)
  • rice
    120 g
  • unwaxed lemon
    1

Serve

  • dill (optional)

🍳 Cookware

  • very large saucepan

Stock Base

  1. 1
    Put the olive oil in a very large saucepan (not frying pan) on a medium-high heat. Fry the chicken pieces until golden all over, and transfer to a plate. Do this in batches, if necessary.
    olive oil: 2 tbsp, chicken: 1 small (jointed, plus carcas)
  2. 2
    Add the garlic and thyme into the hot pan, and fry for ⏱️ 30 seconds . Return all the chicken to the pan. Add the carrot , the onion , the tomato , and the black peppercorns .
    garlic: 1 clove (peeled), thyme: 1 sprig, carrot: 1 (roughly chopped), onion: 1 (roughly chopped), tomato: 1 medium (whole), black peppercorns: 1 tsp
  3. 3
    Cover with water and bring to a boil. Turn down the heat, partially cover the pan, and leave to simmer gently for about ⏱️ 1 hour until the chicken is cooked through. Lift the chicken out of the pan, leave it to cool, then strip the meat. Strain the stock, discard the solids, then return the liquid to the pan.
    water: 2.25 litres

Soup

  1. 1
    Add the rice and diced carrot to the hot stock, bring to a boil, then lower the heat. Simmer, stirring occasionally, for about ⏱️ 5 minutes . Add the leek and simmer for another ⏱️ 8 minutes , until the rice and vegetables are cooked. Turn off the heat.
    rice: 120 g, carrot: 1 (diced), leek: 1 slender (sliced)
  2. 2
    Separate the eggs , and put the yolks in a large heatproof bowl and the whites in a bowl big enough to hold them once beaten. Zest the unwaxed lemon directly into the hot soup, then juice it and beat this into the egg yolks. Beat the whites until they hold soft peaks. Mix the yolks with lemon juice, then fold in the whites.
    eggs: 2, unwaxed lemon: 1
  3. 3
    Gradually whisk in about half the chicken stock from the pot, then whisk everything back into the stock pan. Cook gently for about ⏱️ 5 minutes , without allowing the soup to boil, until the soup thickens slightly. Season to taste, optionally adding more lemon juice , and some of the shredded chicken.
    lemon juice
  4. 📝

    The rest of the chicken can be kept for other purposes.

Serve

  1. 1
    Divide the soup between bowls, scatter with a little chopped dill , if using, and eat immediately.
    dill
  2. 📝

    Because of the eggs, this soup does not freeze well, but it can be reheated successfully as long as it is stirred constantly and not boiled.
