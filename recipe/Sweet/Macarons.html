Macarons (French Style)

.cook
📊 Hard 🍽️ Dessert 🌍 French

🥘 Ingredients

Macaron Shells

  • almond flour (or ground almonds)
    75 g
  • cream of tartar (optional)
    1/8 tsp
  • egg whites (preferably aged)
    2
  • food colouring powder
    1/4 tsp
  • granulated sugar
    50 g
  • icing sugar
    90 g
  • vinegar

Filling: Chocolate and Raspberry

  • butter (room temperature)
    25 g
  • dark chocolate (finely chopped)
    110 g
  • double cream
    120 g
  • raspberry jam
    75 g

Filling: Mint Chocolate

  • butter (room temperature)
    50 g
  • dark chocolate (finely chopped)
    220 g
  • double cream
    240 g
  • peppermint extract
    1/2 tsp

Filling: Blueberry

  • blueberries
    2 per sandwich
  • double cream (whipped)
    100 ml

Filling: Lemon

  • clotted cream (optional)
  • lemon curd
    100 ml

🍳 Cookware

  • piping bag with a round piping tip
  • silicone mats with macron templates

Macaron Shells

  1. 1
    Fit a piping bag with a round piping tip , twist just above the tip, and place in a pint glass.
  2. 2
    Pulse the icing sugar , granulated sugar , and almond flour (or ground almonds) in a food processor about 15 times until fully combined. Sieve the mixture into a large bowl, discarding any particles that stay in the sieve.
    icing sugar: 90 g, granulated sugar: 50 g, almond flour (or ground almonds): 75 g
  3. 3
    Wipe the mixer bowl and whisk with vinegar to remove grease residue. Whisk the egg whites on 4 for 3 to ⏱️ 4 minutes until they start to form soft peaks. Add the cream of tartar , food colouring powder , and icing sugar. Whisk on maximum speed until stiff peaks form, but do not over whisk.
    vinegar, egg whites: 2 (preferably aged), cream of tartar: 1/8 tsp, food colouring powder: 1/4 tsp
  4. 4
    Using a spatula gently mix one third of the meringue into the dry ingredients, by wiping around the edge of the bowl and then cutting through the mixture, until fully combined. Repeat with the next third. Add the final third and gently fold into the mixture. Stop when it is possible to drop a continuous figure eight from the spatula, but before the mixture becomes too runny.
  5. 5
    Transfer the mixture to a piping bag. Pipe onto silicone mats with macron templates . Hold the piping tip vertically about 1/4” above the mat and squeeze gently until the batter fills the inner circle. Move the tip in a small circle whilst lifting the bag vertically to avoid forming a tail on the macaron.
  6. 6
    Bang the tray hard several times against the worktop or floor to knock out any air bubbles. Any visible bubbles can be popped with a toothpick.
  7. 7
    Leave to dry in front of a fan until they form a skin. This will take about 15 to ⏱️ 20 minutes , or significantly longer on rainy/humid days. Do not rush this step or the macarons will not rise or form feet.
  8. 8
    Preheat oven to 145°C. Bake for 12 to ⏱️ 14 minutes until the top of the shell does not wobble.
  9. 9
    Allow the shells to cool completely, e.g. by sliding the mat onto a cold worktop, before peeling off the macarons.
  10. 10
    Pair shells of similar sizes and fill.
  11. 📝

    Place in the fridge for at least 24 hours to mature. Warm at room temperature before serving.

Filling: Chocolate and Raspberry

  1. 1
    Bring the double cream to the boil in a small saucepan. Pour over the dark chocolate and leave to stand for a few minutes. Stir to combine. Add the butter and stir until smooth, then leave to set until thickened. Use a piping bag with a small nozzle to pipe around the edge of one shell. Fill the centre with raspberry jam .
    double cream: 120 g, dark chocolate: 110 g (finely chopped), butter: 25 g (room temperature), raspberry jam: 75 g

Filling: Mint Chocolate

  1. 1
    Bring the double cream and peppermint extract to the boil in a small saucepan. Pour over the dark chocolate and leave to stand for a few minutes. Stir to combine. Add the butter , and stir until smooth, then leave to set until thickened. Use a piping bag with a small nozzle to pipe around the edge of one shell.
    double cream: 240 g, peppermint extract: 1/2 tsp, dark chocolate: 220 g (finely chopped), butter: 50 g (room temperature)

Filling: Blueberry

  1. 1
    Fill with double cream and add two blueberries to each sandwich.
    double cream: 100 ml (whipped), blueberries: 2 per sandwich

Filling: Lemon

  1. 1
    Fill each sandwich with lemon curd and clotted cream .
    lemon curd: 100 ml, clotted cream
