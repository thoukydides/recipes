Baklava

.cook
#greek #sweet #dessert #snack #vegetarian

A rich sweet pastry made from layers of buttered filo dough filled with chopped nuts, and soaked in syrup

📊 Medium 🍽️ Dessert ⏱️ Prep Time: 45m 🔥 Cook Time: 1h15m 🌍 Greek 🥗 Vegetarian 👤 Tess Mallos 📖 The Complete Middle East Cookbook <urn:isbn:0727103555>

🥘 Ingredients

Baklava

  • almonds (finely chopped)
    235 ml
  • cardamon seeds
    2 pods
  • caster sugar
    4 tbsp
  • filo pastry
    20 sheets
  • ground cinnamon
    2 tsp
  • ground cloves
    1/8 tsp
  • unsalted butter (melted)
    175 g
  • walnuts (finely chopped)
    470 ml

Syrup

  • cinnamon bark
    small piece
  • cloves
    3
  • honey
    4 tbsp
  • lemon juice
    2 tsp
  • lemon rind (thinly peeled)
    1 strip
  • sugar
    355 ml
  • water
    355 ml

🍳 Cookware

  • 33×23×5 cm (13×9×2 inch) oven dish
  • heavy pan

Baklava

  1. 📝

    Can use pistachios too. Add 2 tsp of cinnamon to a walnut filling and the seeds of 2 cardamon pods to an almond one.

  2. 1
    Mix walnuts , almonds , caster sugar , ground cinnamon , ground cloves , and cardamon seeds .
    walnuts: 470 ml (finely chopped), almonds: 235 ml (finely chopped), caster sugar: 4 tbsp, ground cinnamon: 2 tsp, ground cloves: 1/8 tsp, cardamon seeds: 2 pods
  3. 2
    Butter base and sides of 33×23×5 cm (13×9×2 inch) oven dish and place nine sheets of filo pastry separately into dish, brushing each with unsalted butter .
    filo pastry: 20 sheets, unsalted butter: 175 g (melted)
  4. 3
    Spread half of the nut mixture over the filo. Top with another two sheets of filo, brushing each with butter.
  5. 4
    Spread remaining nuts on top and finish with remaining filo, brushing each sheet as before.
  6. 5
    Trim edges and brush top with butter. Cut baklava with a sharp knife into diamond shapes. Sprinkle lightly with water to prevent top layers curling upwards.
  7. 6
    Bake on centre shelf in a moderately slow oven (160°C) for ⏱️ 30 minutes . Move up one shelf and cook for further {30%minutes}. Cover with greased brown paper or foil if top colours too quickly. Pastry must be allowed to cook thoroughly.

Syrup

  1. 📝

    Halve quantities for a less sugary taste. Alternative syrup ingredients: 300 ml water, 500 g sugar, squeeze of lemon juice and 2 tbsp rose water.

  2. 1
    When baklava goes into the oven, make the syrup. Place sugar , water and honey in a heavy pan and stir over medium heat until sugar is dissolved. Add remaining syrup ingredients ( lemon rind , cinnamon bark , cloves , and lemon juice ), bring to the boil and boil for ⏱️ 15 minutes . Strain and cool.
    sugar: 355 ml, water: 355 ml, honey: 4 tbsp, lemon rind: 1 strip (thinly peeled), cinnamon bark: small piece, cloves: 3, lemon juice: 2 tsp
  3. 2
    Spoon syrup evenly over hot baklava. Leave for ⏱️ 2 hours before cutting again into serving portions.
