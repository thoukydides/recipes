Hot Cross Buns

.cook
📊 Medium 🍽️ Dessert 🌍 British

🥘 Ingredients

Starter Sponge

  • caster sugar
    1 tsp
  • egg (beaten)
    1
  • fresh yeast (or 7 g dried yeast)
    20 g
  • lukewarm water
    100 ml
  • plain flour
    55 g

Dough

  • baking powder
    1 tsp
  • black tea (earl gray preferred)
    1 cup
  • butter (melted)
    85 g
  • caster sugar
    85 g
  • ground cardammon
    1 tsp
  • ground mixed spice
    40 g
  • milk
    120 ml
  • mixed dried fruit (such as sultanas, raisins, and chopped mixed peel; avoid currants; try dried apricots, unsweetened cranberries, figs, or tart morello cherries)
    255 g
  • plain flour
    455 g
  • salt
    1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • greased baking tray with deep sides

Starter Sponge

  1. 📝

    Tesco sell fresh yeast in multiples of 100 g.

  2. 1
    In a small bowl combine the lukewarm water , egg , fresh yeast , plain flour , and caster sugar . Cover and leave in a warm draught-free place for about half an hour (or longer if it is not puffy by then).
    lukewarm water: 100 ml, egg: 1 (beaten), fresh yeast: 20 g (or 7 g dried yeast), plain flour: 55 g, caster sugar: 1 tsp

Dough

  1. 1
    Place the mixed dried fruit in another small bowl and cover with the boiling black tea (earl gray preferred) . Leave for ⏱️ 20 minutes .
    mixed dried fruit: 255 g (such as sultanas, raisins, and chopped mixed peel; avoid currants; try dried apricots, unsweetened cranberries, figs, or tart morello cherries), black tea (earl gray preferred): 1 cup
  2. 2
    Mix the dry ingredients for the dough ( plain flour , ground mixed spice , ground cardammon , caster sugar , salt , and baking powder ) in a large bowl. Add the milk and butter to the dry dough ingredients. Mix until it begins to come together, then add in the starter sponge and mix well. The dough will be incredibly sticky at this point. Drain the fruit well and sit aside.
    plain flour: 455 g, ground mixed spice: 40 g, ground cardammon: 1 tsp, caster sugar: 85 g, salt: 1 tsp, baking powder: 1 tsp, milk: 120 ml, butter: 85 g (melted)
  3. 3
    Liberally flour the worktop and knead the dough until it is soft and springy. It will take in about quarter to third of a cup of flour during kneading. Flatten the dough into a rough rectangle, sprinkle the fruit over the top, and knead it in briefly, until evenly distributed. Once soft enough to handle, place it in a clean greased bowl and cover with clingfilm. Place it in a warm draught-free spot and allow it to rise for about ⏱️ 1 hour until it has doubled in size.
  4. 4
    Knead the dough and return it to the bowl for a further ⏱️ 30 minutes .
  5. 5
    Once the dough has risen again, knock it back just enough to work with it and divide the dough into 12 pieces. Roll the pieces into balls and spread them out in a greased baking tray with deep sides . Set aside for ⏱️ 30 minutes in the warm draught-free spot. Heat the oven to 200°C.
  6. 6
    Bake for 20 to ⏱️ 25 minutes . If the buns are browning too quickly then cover with foil and reduce the heat to 180°C.
