Pear and Almond Cream Tart

.cook
#vegetarian #dessert #tart #sweet #french

An indulgent dessert

👥 6 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Dessert ⏱️ Prep Time: 45m 🔥 Cook Time: 55m 🌍 French 🥗 Vegetarian 👤 Catherine Atkinson 📖 Best-Ever Pastry Cookbook <urn:isbn:1843094703>

🥘 Ingredients

Pastry

  • butter (diced)
    100 g
  • chilled water
    1 tbsp
  • egg yolks
    2
  • icing sugar
    25 g
  • plain flour
    200 g
  • salt
    pinch

Almond Cream Filling

  • almond essence
    few drops
  • blanched almonds
    100 g
  • butter
    65 g
  • caster sugar
    50 g
  • egg
    1
  • egg white
    1

Assemble and Bake

  • firm pears
    3
  • lemon juice
    a little
  • peach brandy or water
    15 ml
  • peach preserve (strained)
    60 ml

🍳 Cookware

  • 23cm/9in flan tin (quiche pan)

Pastry

  1. 1
    To make the pastry, sift the plain flour with the salt and icing sugar . Rub or cut in the butter until the mixture resembles fine breadcrumbs. Mix the egg yolks and chilled water , sprinkle over the dry ingredients and mix to a dough. Knead for a few seconds until smooth. Wrap in clingfilm and chill for ⏱️ 30 minutes .
    plain flour: 200 g, salt: pinch, icing sugar: 25 g, butter: 100 g (diced), egg yolks: 2, chilled water: 1 tbsp
  2. 2
    Roll out the pastry and use to line a 23cm/9in flan tin (quiche pan) , then chill.

Almond Cream Filling

  1. 1
    Meanwhile, make the filling. Pulse the blanched almonds and caster sugar in a food processor until finely ground but not pasty. Add the butter and process until creamy, then mix in the egg , egg white and almond essence .
    blanched almonds: 100 g, caster sugar: 50 g, butter: 65 g, egg: 1, egg white: 1, almond essence: few drops

Assemble and Bake

  1. 1
    Place a baking sheet in the oven and preheat to 190°C. Thinly peel and halve the firm pears , remove their cores and rub them lightly with lemon juice . Put them cut side down on a chopping board and slice thinly crossways, keeping the slices together.
    firm pears: 3, lemon juice: a little
  2. 2
    Pour the almond cream filling into the pastry case. Slide a palette knife under one pear half and press the top to fan out the slices. Quickly transfer to the tart, placing the fruit on the filling like spokes of a wheel. Remove a few slices from each half before arranging and use to fill in any gaps in the centre.
  3. 3
    Place the tart on the hot baking sheet and bake for 50 to ⏱️ 55 minutes , or until the filling is set and well browned. Cool on a wire rack.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, heat the peach brandy or water and the peach preserve in a small pan, then brush over the top of the hot tart to glaze.
    peach brandy or water: 15 ml, peach preserve: 60 ml (strained)
  5. 📝

    Serve at room temperature.
