Galaktoboureko

.cook
#vegetarian #greek #sweet #dessert

Greek filo pastry custard pie, in the form of individual rolls

📊 Medium 🍽️ Dessert ⏱️ Prep Time: 1h 🔥 Cook Time: 50m 🌍 Greek 🥗 Vegetarian

🥘 Ingredients

  • filo sheets (room temperature)
    900 g

Syrup

  • caster sugar
    400 g
  • cinnamon
    1 stick
  • lemon juice
    2 tbsp
  • lemon peel
    2 slices
  • water
    475 ml

Custard

  • caster sugar
    100 g
  • eggs (room temperature)
    3
  • lemon juice
    1/2 tbsp
  • lemon rind (grated)
    1 tsp
  • semolina
    85 g
  • unsalted butter (melted)
    240 g
  • whole milk
    700 ml

🍳 Cookware

  • small saucepan
  • electric mixer
  • large saucepan
  • shallow baking pan
  1. 1
    Remove the filo sheets from the fridge to come to room temperature (but do not open).
    filo sheets: 900 g (room temperature)

Syrup

  1. 1
    Make the syrup by combining the caster sugar and water in a small saucepan . Bring to a boil over medium heat.
    caster sugar: 400 g, water: 475 ml
  2. 2
    Add the lemon peel and cinnamon . Reduce the heat to low and cook for ⏱️ 10 minutes .
    lemon peel: 2 slices, cinnamon: 1 stick
  3. 3
    Remove the syrup from heat, add the lemon juice (but do not stir) and set aside to cool.
    lemon juice: 2 tbsp

Custard

  1. 1
    With an electric mixer on high speed beat one eggs with a third of the caster sugar until light and fluffy. Add another egg and another third of the sugar, and repeat until all three eggs and all the sugar have been beaten together. Continue to beat on high for ⏱️ 2 minutes .
    eggs: 3 (room temperature), caster sugar: 100 g
  2. 2
    Add the semolina , lemon juice , and lemon rind . Beat until mixed.
    semolina: 85 g, lemon juice: 1/2 tbsp, lemon rind: 1 tsp (grated)
  3. 3
    Add the whole milk and 1 tbsp of the unsalted butter . Beat for another 5 to ⏱️ 6 minutes until the mixture is light and fluffy.​
    whole milk: 700 ml, unsalted butter: 240 g (melted)
  4. 4
    Transfer the custard to a large saucepan and heat over medium heat. Whisk vigorously until it thickens to the consistency of ketchup (or puréed baby food). Transfer to a cold bowl and set aside.​

Assemble

  1. 1
    Brush the bottom and sides of a shallow baking pan with melted butter.
  2. 2
    Open the filo dough and work with 4 or 5 sheets at a time, keeping the rest covered with a damp towel.
  3. 3
    Fold each sheet of filo in half along its long edge. Brush the top lightly with melted butter.
  4. 4
    Place about 1 tbsp of the custard along the bottom edge, about 1” from the bottom and sides. Do not overfill.
  5. 5
    Fold the filo up over the custard from the bottom, and roll up two or three times, trying to make sure there is no air space between the pastry and the custard.
  6. 6
    Fold the sides in to create a straight channel and roll the pastry up the rest of the way, keeping the roll tidy but not too tight as the custard will expand during cooking.
  7. 7
    Place each roll in the baking pan with the seam side down. Do not place too closely together.
  8. 8
    Brush the tops and sides of the rolls with melted butter.

Bake

  1. 1
    Bake at 175°C for around ⏱️ 35 minutes , until golden.
  2. 2
    Remove the cinnamon stick and lemon peel from the syrup and pour over the pastries. Leave to sit for several hours until the pastries have reached room temperature and the syrup has been absorbed.
  3. 📝

    Store in an airtight container in the fridge. Eat within two days.
