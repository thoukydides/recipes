Tarte au Citron

.cook
👥 8 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Dessert 🌍 French

🥘 Ingredients

Pastry

  • butter (cold, small cubes)
    100 g
  • cold water
    1 tbsp
  • egg yolk
    1
  • icing sugar
    25 g
  • plain flour
    175 g

Filling

  • caster sugar
    225 g
  • double cream
    125 ml
  • eggs
    5
  • lemons (juice and zest)
    4

Serve

  • icing sugar

🍳 Cookware

  • 23cm/9” loose-bottomed fluted tart tin
  • baking beans
  • blow torch

Pastry

  1. 1
    Place the plain flour , butter and icing sugar into a food processor. Pulse briefly until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.
    plain flour: 175 g, butter: 100 g (cold, small cubes), icing sugar: 25 g
  2. 2
    Add the egg yolk and cold water . Pulse again until the mixture sticks together in clumps. Tip onto a work surface and gather into a ball by hand. Knead the pastry two or three times to make it smooth. If the pastry is too soft then wrap it in parchment paper and chill for ⏱️ 15 minutes .
    egg yolk: 1, cold water: 1 tbsp
  3. 3
    Grease a 23cm/9” loose-bottomed fluted tart tin .
  4. 4
    Lay a piece of parchment paper on the work surface. Remove the base from the tart tin and lay it on the paper. Using a pencil draw a circle onto the paper 4cm/1½” bigger than the tin base.
  5. 5
    Dust the base of the tin with flour. Place the pastry ball in the centre of the tin base and flatten it out slightly. Roll out the pastry, still on the base, until it meets the circle mark. Turn the pastry while rolling by turning the paper. Gently fold the pastry surrounding the tin base in towards the centre.
  6. 6
    Carefully lift the tin base off the work surface, drop into the tin, then ease the pastry into the corners and up the sides of the tin, pressing the overhang lightly over the rim. If the pastry has cracked at all, simply press it together to seal. Press the pastry into the flutes of the tin then lightly prick the base with a fork, but not quite all of the way through. Place the pastry-lined tin on a baking tray, cover loosely with cling film and chill in the fridge for ⏱️ 30 minutes . Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  7. 7
    Remove the cling film from the pastry case and line with foil so it supports the sides, then fill with the baking beans . Bake blind for 10 to ⏱️ 12 minutes , until the pastry is set.
  8. 8
    Lift out the foil and beans. Carefully trim the excess pastry from the sides using a sharp knife. Return the empty pastry case to the oven for another 10 to ⏱️ 12 minutes or until it is pale golden and completely dry. Set aside to cool while making the filling. Reduce the oven temperature to 170°C.

Filling

  1. 1
    Break the eggs into a large bowl and whisk together. Add the double cream , caster sugar , and lemons , and whisk again until they are well combined. Pour the filling mixture into a jug, then into the cooled baked pastry case. To prevent it spilling as it goes in the oven, pour in most of the filling so it almost fills the tart, carefully sit the baking sheet and tart on the oven shelf, then top up with the rest of the filling to completely fill it. Bake for about 30 to ⏱️ 35 minutes or until just set with a slight wobble in the centre.
    eggs: 5, double cream: 125 ml, caster sugar: 225 g, lemons: 4 (juice and zest)
  2. 2
    Leave to cool slightly then, when the pastry seems firm enough, remove the tart from the tin and transfer to a serving plate.

Serve

  1. 1
    Can be made up to two days in advance. Decorate just before serving by dusting with sifted icing sugar . To glaze the surface hold a blow torch flame just above the sugar and move it around until the sugar has caramelised, being careful not to burn the pastry edges. Serve warm or cold.
