Stollen

.cook
👥 12 servings 📊 Hard 🍽️ Dessert 🌍 German

🥘 Ingredients

  • Marzipan (preferably soft paste)
    225 g
  • almond extract
    2 drops
  • almonds, blanched (finely chopped)
    55 g
  • candied peel
    125 g
  • caster sugar
    100 g
  • currants
    100 g
  • fresh yeast (or 10 g dried yeast)
    28 g
  • ground cloves
    pinch
  • ground nutmeg
    pinch
  • raisins
    200 g
  • salt
    10 g
  • strong white flour
    500 g
  • unsalted butter (softened, melted)
    175 g
  • vanilla extract
    1/2 tsp
  • whole milk
    250 ml

Serve

  • icing sugar
    2 tbsp
  • unsalted butter (melted)
    25 g
  1. 📝

    Tesco sell fresh yeast in multiples of 100 g.

  2. 1
    Put the strong white flour and caster sugar in a large bowl. Add the fresh yeast on one side of the bowl and the salt on the other. Add the unsalted butter and 200ml of the whole milk and stir together.
    strong white flour: 500 g, caster sugar: 100 g, fresh yeast: 28 g (or 10 g dried yeast), salt: 10 g, unsalted butter: 150 g (softened), whole milk: 250 ml
  3. 2
    Add the remaining milk and bring the mixture together to form a soft dough. Transfer to a generously floured work surface and knead for 6 to ⏱️ 7 minutes , or until smooth and pliable.
  4. 3
    Mix the ground nutmeg , ground cloves , vanilla extract , almond extract , almonds, blanched , raisins , currants , and candied peel together in a large bowl. Put the dough on top and knead from the outside into the centre incorporating the dried fruit and spices. When everything has been fully incorporated, cover the bowl with cling film and leave to rise for 1 to ⏱️ 2 hours in a warm place, or until doubled in size.
    ground nutmeg: pinch, ground cloves: pinch, vanilla extract: 1/2 tsp, almond extract: 2 drops, almonds, blanched: 55 g (finely chopped), raisins: 200 g, currants: 100 g, candied peel: 125 g
  5. 4
    Line a baking tray with parchment.
  6. 5
    Flatten the dough and roll out on a lightly floured work surface into a rectangle about 35✕45cm (or so the short side of the dough matches the longest side of the baking tray). Brush with the unsalted butter .
    unsalted butter: 25 g (melted)
  7. 6
    If the Marzipan is soft enough then spread it over the dough, leaving a gap at the bottom and top. Otherwise roll out the marzipan to about 35✕15cm (the long side should approximately match the width of the dough), and then place on top of the dough in the middle.
    Marzipan: 225 g (preferably soft paste)
  8. 7
    Roll the dough up to enclose the marzipan and transfer to a baking tray lined with baking parchment. Cover and leave to rise for about ⏱️ 1 hour , or until risen and doubled in size.
  9. 8
    When the dough is risen, preheat the oven to 180°C and bake for ⏱️ 15 minutes .
  10. 9
    Reduce the heat to 130°C and bake for a further ⏱️ 45 minutes .

Serve

  1. 1
    Remove from the oven, brush with unsalted butter , and dust with icing sugar . Serve cold.
    unsalted butter: 25 g (melted), icing sugar: 2 tbsp
