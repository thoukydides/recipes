Semolina and Nut Halva

.cook
👥 10 servings 📊 Hard 🍽️ Dessert

🥘 Ingredients

Halva

  • baking powder
    2 tsp
  • caster sugar
    115 g
  • eggs
    3
  • ground hazelnuts
    115 g
  • orange (finely grated rind)
    1
  • orange juice
    2 tbsp
  • semolina
    175 g
  • unsalted butter (softened)
    115 g

Finish

  • almonds (blanched) (toasted and chopped)
    50 g
  • caster sugar
    350 g
  • cinnamon sticks (halved)
    2
  • hazelnuts (unblanched) (toasted and chopped)
    50 g
  • lemon juice
    2 tbsp
  • orange (shredded rind of)
    1
  • orange blossom water
    4 tbsp
  • water
    575 ml

Serve

  • clotted cream (or lightly whipped cream)

🍳 Cookware

  • deep 23cm/9" square solid-base cake tin
  • small heavy-based saucepan

Halva

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 220°C. Grease and line the base of a deep 23cm/9" square solid-base cake tin .
  2. 2
    Lightly cream the unsalted butter in a bowl. Add the caster sugar , orange and orange juice , eggs , semolina , baking powder , and ground hazelnuts , and beat the ingredients together until smooth.
    unsalted butter: 115 g (softened), caster sugar: 115 g, orange: 1 (finely grated rind), orange juice: 2 tbsp, eggs: 3, semolina: 175 g, baking powder: 2 tsp, ground hazelnuts: 115 g
  3. 3
    Turn into the prepared tin and level the surface. Bake for 15 to ⏱️ 20 minutes until just firm and golden. Leave to cool in the tin.

Finish

  1. 1
    To make the syrup, put the caster sugar in a small heavy-based saucepan with water and the cinnamon sticks . Heat gently, stirring, until the sugar has dissolved completely.
    caster sugar: 350 g, water: 575 ml, cinnamon sticks: 2 (halved)
  2. 2
    Bring to the boil and boil fast, without stirring, for ⏱️ 5 minutes . Measure half the boiling syrup and add the lemon juice and orange blossom water to it. Pour over the halva. Reserve the remainder of the syrup in the pan.
    lemon juice: 2 tbsp, orange blossom water: 4 tbsp
  3. 3
    Leave the halva in the tin until the syrup is absorbed then turn it out on to a plate and cut diagonally into diamond-shaped portions. Scatter with the hazelnuts (unblanched) and almonds (blanched) .
    hazelnuts (unblanched): 50 g (toasted and chopped), almonds (blanched): 50 g (toasted and chopped)
  4. 4
    Boil the remaining syrup until slightly thickened (but not too much otherwise it will set hard) then pour it over the halva. Scatter the orange over the cake.
    orange: 1 (shredded rind of)

Serve

  1. 1
    Serve with clotted cream .
    clotted cream (or lightly whipped cream)
