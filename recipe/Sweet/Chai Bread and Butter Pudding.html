Chai Bread and Butter Pudding

.cook
👥 6 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Dessert

🥘 Ingredients

  • Indian leaf tea, preferably Assam
    2 tbsp
  • brioche loaf (thickly sliced, or 10 slices of crustless white bread, thickly cut)
    300 g
  • butter (softened)
    50 g
  • caster sugar
    150 g, 1 tbsp
  • cinnamon stick (broken in half)
    1
  • cloves
    8
  • double cream
    300 ml
  • egg yolks
    4 large
  • flaked sea salt
    1/2 tsp
  • green cardamom pods
    15
  • ground ginger
    1 1/2 tsp
  • mixed dried fruits
    125 g
  • nutmeg (half a whole nutmeg freshly grated)
    1 tsp
  • orange peel
    1 long strip
  • vanilla extract
    1 tsp
  • whole eggs
    2 large
  • whole milk
    400 ml

🍳 Cookware

  • medium nonstick saucepan
  • 1.75-2 litre shallow ovenproof dish
  1. 1
    Put the green cardamom pods and cloves in a pestle and mortar and pound for about a minute, or until the pods are split and the cloves are roughly crushed. Tip into a medium nonstick saucepan and add the nutmeg , ground ginger , flaked sea salt and whole milk . Stir in the caster sugar , vanilla extract , Indian leaf tea, preferably Assam , orange peel , and cinnamon stick . Bring to a gentle simmer and cook over a low heat for ⏱️ 5 minutes , stirring occasionally. Remove the pan from the heat and leave to stand for ⏱️ 10 minutes .
    green cardamom pods: 15, cloves: 8, nutmeg: 1 tsp (half a whole nutmeg freshly grated), ground ginger: 1 1/2 tsp, flaked sea salt: 1/2 tsp, whole milk: 400 ml, caster sugar: 150 g, vanilla extract: 1 tsp, Indian leaf tea, preferably Assam: 2 tbsp, orange peel: 1 long strip, cinnamon stick: 1 (broken in half)
  2. 2
    Butter a 1.75-2 litre shallow ovenproof dish (a round one looks good). Spread one side of each slice of brioche loaf with butter . Arrange the brioche slices in the dish, butter side up and slightly upright but leaning against each other, scattering the mixed dried fruits between the slices.
    brioche loaf: 300 g (thickly sliced, or 10 slices of crustless white bread, thickly cut), butter: 50 g (softened), mixed dried fruits: 125 g
  3. 3
    Whisk the egg yolks , whole eggs and double cream together in a large jug or bowl until smooth. Strain the infused milk through a fine sieve onto the egg mixture and stir well to combine. Discard the spices and peel.
    egg yolks: 4 large, whole eggs: 2 large, double cream: 300 ml
  4. 4
    Pour the creamy liquid slowly over the buttered brioche. (The bread will float, so press it down gently with a spatula for a few seconds to help it absorb the custard.) Leave to stand for ⏱️ 15 minutes .
  5. 5
    Preheat the oven to 180ºC.
  6. 6
    Place the dish in a roasting tin. Pour boiled water into the roasting tin around the outside of dish to come half way up the sides. Sprinkle the pudding with caster sugar and bake for 35 to ⏱️ 40 minutes , or until the custard is just set, but still just a little creamy in the centre, and the bread is golden-brown and crisp on top.
    caster sugar: 1 tbsp
  7. 7
    Remove from the oven and carefully lift out of the roasting tin.
  8. 📝

    Serve hot with cream, custard or good vanilla ice cream.
