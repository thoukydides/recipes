Chocolate Roulade

.cook
📊 Medium 🍽️ Dessert 📖 www.theguardian.com

🥘 Ingredients

Meringue

  • caster sugar
    300 g
  • cocoa powder
    50 g
  • cream of tartar (or ½ tsp vinegar or lemon juice)
    1/4 tsp
  • dark chocolate shavings / chips
    50 g
  • egg whites
    5 large
  • mixed spice
    1 tsp
  • salt
    pinch

Filling

  • chestnut purée (sweetened to taste with icing sugar)
    200 g
  • double cream
    250 ml
  • fruit, e.g. cherries in kirsch
    handful

🍳 Cookware

  • shallow (roughly 22 x 30 cm) oven tray

Meringue

  1. 1
    Heat the oven to 140°C. Line a shallow (roughly 22 x 30 cm) oven tray with greaseproof paper.
  2. 2
    Sift the cocoa powder and mixed spice into a bowl and set aside. Chop the dark chocolate shavings / chips into chips, if necessary.
    cocoa powder: 50 g, mixed spice: 1 tsp, dark chocolate shavings / chips: 50 g
  3. 3
    Put the egg whites in a large bowl with the salt and cream of tartar , and whisk to soft peaks. Gradually whisk in the caster sugar until the meringue mix is thick and glossy and falls in ribbons off the whisk.
    egg whites: 5 large, salt: pinch, cream of tartar: 1/4 tsp (or ½ tsp vinegar or lemon juice), caster sugar: 300 g
  4. 4
    Add the cocoa and most of the chocolate chips, then fold in until well combined, taking care not to knock out too much air.
  5. 5
    Spoon the meringue onto the lined tray in a single layer and gently smooth the top, to level it out, while keeping in as much air as possible.
  6. 6
    Scatter the remaining chocolate all over the top, then bake for about 30 to ⏱️ 40 minutes , until the meringue is dry to the touch on top but still squidgy beneath. Remove and leave to cool on a tray.

Filling

  1. 1
    Once the meringue is cool, start the filling. Beat the double cream until it is billowing and soft, then fold in the chestnut purée and taste. It should not be too sweet because it will be paired with the meringue.
    double cream: 250 ml, chestnut purée: 200 g (sweetened to taste with icing sugar)
  2. 2
    Put a large sheet of clingfilm on a work surface and dust all over with cocoa powder. Carefully turn the meringue out onto it, with one of the short sides facing you. Score a shallow cut across the width of the meringue, about 2 cm from the closest end.
  3. 3
    Spread the cream all over the top of the meringue. Scatter over the fruit, e.g. cherries in kirsch , putting a line of fruit roughly along the scored cut.
    fruit, e.g. cherries in kirsch: handful
  4. 4
    Using the clingfilm to help, tuck the closest end of the meringue over the line of fruit, then roll the whole thing up as tightly as possible, finishing with the seam underneath. The roulade should be completely encased in clingfilm. Refrigerated for at least ⏱️ 2 hours , but it tastes better the following day.
  5. 5
    Transfer the roulade seam side down onto a platter, unwrap carefully. Dust with more cocoa powder and serve.
