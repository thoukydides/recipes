Tart au Fruit Rouge

.cook
👥 6 servings 📊 Medium 🍽️ Dessert 🌍 French

🥘 Ingredients

Sweet Pastry

  • egg yolks
    2
  • icing sugar
    75 g
  • plain flour
    250 g
  • unsalted butter
    175 g
  • water
    25 ml

Crème Pâtissière

  • egg yolks
    2
  • eggs
    2
  • milk
    300 ml
  • plain flour
    55 g
  • sugar
    75 g
  • vanilla pod (halved lengthways)
    1

Assemble and Bake

  • fresh mint
  • icing sugar
  • mixture of red berries

🍳 Cookware

  • heavy-based pan
  • tart case
  • baking beans

Sweet Pastry

  1. 1
    Cream the unsalted butter and icing sugar together, add the egg yolks and mix. Add the plain flour and combine, then add the water and mix well. The pastry will look sticky and you might think that something has gone wrong; but that is fine. Wrap in cling film and refrigerate for at least ⏱️ 1 hour before use.
    unsalted butter: 175 g, icing sugar: 75 g, egg yolks: 2, plain flour: 250 g, water: 25 ml

Crème Pâtissière

  1. 1
    Pour the milk and vanilla pod in a heavy-based pan and bring to the boil. In a bowl, mix the eggs , egg yolks , and sugar , then add plain flour . When the milk has boiled, slowly pour over the egg mixture stirring constantly. Pour back into the pan and bring back to the boil. Turn the heat down and cook for ⏱️ 2 minutes stirring constantly. Remove the vanilla pod, pour into another container to stop the cooking process, cover with cling film and leave to cool down before using.
    milk: 300 ml, vanilla pod: 1 (halved lengthways), eggs: 2, egg yolks: 2, sugar: 75 g, plain flour: 55 g
  2. 📝

    For a more delicate flavour you can fold some whipped cream into the crème.

Assemble and Bake

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Grease the tart case unless it is non-stick.
  2. 2
    Roll out the pastry on a lightly floured surface, to a size large enough to cover your tart case base and sides. Gently place the pastry into the tin making sure to ease the dough into the corners of the tin, for a more perfect result leave a little of the pastry overhanging the sides (if presentation is not paramount, trim the excess pastry from raw, you will be able to make extra tarts).
  3. 3
    Line the tart with greaseproof paper and fill with enough baking beans to ensure that the sides will keep their shape during cooking. Bake for ⏱️ 10 minutes . Remove the beans and paper (trim the sides if you left the pastry overhanging) and cook for a further 10 to ⏱️ 15 minutes . When cooked, remove from the oven and leave to cool down.
  4. 4
    Delicately fill the case with the crème patissiere and garnish with the mixture of red berries and fresh mint . Dust with a little icing sugar before serving.
    mixture of red berries, fresh mint, icing sugar
