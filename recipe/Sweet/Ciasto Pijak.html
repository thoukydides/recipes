Ciasto Pijak (Drunkard's Cake)

.cook
📊 Hard 🍽️ Dessert 🌍 Polish

🥘 Ingredients

Chocolate Sponge

  • cocoa powder
    2 tbsp
  • cornflour
    1 tbsp
  • eggs (room temperature)
    3 large
  • plain flour
    35 g
  • salt
    pinch
  • sugar
    75 g

Poppyseed Sponge

  • cornflour
    1 tbsp
  • eggs (room temperature)
    3 large
  • flaked coconut
    50 g
  • plain flour
    40 g
  • poppyseeds
    70 g
  • salt
    pinch
  • sugar
    75 g

Creme Patisserie

  • butter
    10 g
  • cornflour
    1 tbsp
  • egg yolks
    5
  • milk
    425 ml
  • plain flour
    2 tbsp
  • sugar
    110 g
  • vanilla pod
    1

Assemble

  • cherry compote preserve
    300 g
  • dark rum or vodka
    250 ml
  • semi-sweet chocolate
    200 g
  • sponge (boudoir) fingers
    1 pack
  • vegetable oil
    1 tsp
  • walnuts (chopped)
    60 g

🍳 Cookware

  • 9″ by 9″ tin
  • handheld whisk
  • saucepan

Chocolate Sponge

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Grease and line a 9″ by 9″ tin .
  2. 2
    Make the chocolate sponge by placing the eggs and sugar in a bowl and beating over a pan of simmering water for ⏱️ 1 minute , until the sugar has dissolved. Using a handheld whisk , beat for about ⏱️ 3 minutes until the mixture has tripled in volume.
    eggs: 3 large (room temperature), sugar: 75 g
  3. 3
    Sift the cocoa powder , plain flour , cornflour and salt into the bowl, folding gently to just incorporate.
    cocoa powder: 2 tbsp, plain flour: 35 g, cornflour: 1 tbsp, salt: pinch
  4. 4
    Spread the mixture in the tin and bake for about ⏱️ 12 minutes until cooked. Remove and allow to cool for ⏱️ 10 minutes before removing from the tin and leaving to cool completely.

Poppyseed Sponge

  1. 1
    Prepare the poppyseed sponge using the same method, beating the eggs and sugar together before sifting in the dry ingredients ( plain flour , cornflour , salt , poppyseeds , and flaked coconut ).
    eggs: 3 large (room temperature), sugar: 75 g, plain flour: 40 g, cornflour: 1 tbsp, salt: pinch, poppyseeds: 70 g, flaked coconut: 50 g
  2. 2
    Bake for ⏱️ 12 minutes or until done before cooling for ⏱️ 10 minutes and removing from the tin.

Creme Patisserie

  1. 1
    Put the milk and vanilla pod in a saucepan and bring to the boil. Take it off the heat and pour it into a bowl, allowing the vanilla pod to infuse the milk.
    milk: 425 ml, vanilla pod: 1
  2. 2
    Mix together the sugar and egg yolks until the mixture is pale and thick. Remove the vanilla pod from the milk and discard. Slowly add the milk to the mixture, whisking constantly.
    sugar: 110 g, egg yolks: 5
  3. 3
    Add the butter , plain flour and cornflour , continuing to whisk the mixture. Return to the pan and bring to the boil, before reducing the heat and simmering the mixture for ⏱️ 3 minutes , stirring continuously.
    butter: 10 g, plain flour: 2 tbsp, cornflour: 1 tbsp
  4. 📝

    Be careful; the creme patisserie burns easily, and quickly becomes too thick to spread easily.

  5. 4
    Remove the mixture from the heat immediately and place into a bowl, covering with a sheet of greased baking parchment to prevent a skin from forming. Leave to cool completely.

Assemble

  1. 1
    Place the chocolate sponge on the serving dish and spread the cherry compote preserve . Lay the poppyseed cake on top and spread the pastry cream. Place in the fridge to chill for at least ⏱️ 1 hour .
    cherry compote preserve: 300 g
  2. 2
    Dip each sponge (boudoir) fingers in dark rum or vodka before placing them neatly in a single layer on top of the cake. Return to the fridge and chill for another ⏱️ 1 hour .
    sponge (boudoir) fingers: 1 pack, dark rum or vodka: 250 ml
  3. 3
    Melt the semi-sweet chocolate and vegetable oil together and spread over the top of the cooled cake before sprinkling with the walnuts . Put back in the fridge overnight, and serve cold, cut into small squares.
    semi-sweet chocolate: 200 g, vegetable oil: 1 tsp, walnuts: 60 g (chopped)
